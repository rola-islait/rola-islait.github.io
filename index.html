About me

I currently work as an AI Engineer at TrAIngle AI, where my work spans machine-learning research and engineering, full-stack development, and platform work, taking AI-driven software from idea to a secure, working product. Day to day that means moving between research and production: prototyping models, building the interfaces and backend systems around them, hardening security, and writing the documentation that keeps a growing codebase understandable. I like sitting close to both ends of that pipeline: the research questions that shape what a system should do, and the engineering discipline that makes it reliable enough to ship.

Alongside that, I'm a driven student passionate about artificial intelligence and machine learning, with a growing portfolio that spans competitive data science and published research. I'm the winner of the WiDS Global Datathon 2026 (High School Track), the first high school student in Jordan to receive this international recognition, and co-author of an accepted research paper applying SciBERT and Graph Neural Networks to detect AI-generated scientific content. I've also completed a wide range of specialized courses in deep learning, computer vision, generative AI, and reinforcement learning from institutions including Stanford University, DeepLearning.AI, IBM, Meta, and the University of Michigan.

TrAIngle AI is where I get to turn that research energy into real systems, working alongside the amazing founder, Maen Horani.

Research

My research sits at the intersection of NLP and graph learning, and I care about making it possible to tell trustworthy scientific writing apart from AI-generated content.

  • ResearchNet: a novel framework combining SciBERT embeddings with Graph Neural Networks to detect AI-generated scientific text, accepted for presentation at AEECT 2026 and to be published in IEEE Xplore, indexed in Scopus.
  • IEEE Jordan Student Paper Contest: selected as one of 33 accepted papers out of 110 submissions (top 30% acceptance rate).
